What Astra and Sol said

  • Both: v1's lines were too long, and "the bank is slow" didn't explain the double charge. The real cause is that the first payment goes through but its reply is lost. Rewritten.
  • Both: end with you talking to Astra, not a third agent. Adding Sol means another trip through the picker and another name to learn, and Sol fixing refunds brought in a second bug before the first one had settled.
  • Both: Opus declared "That's it" too fast. Now it proves the cause with a failing test before fixing it.
  • Astra: Opus's "I need another angle" read like a cue for the picker. Opus now just reports what it checked. The "@opus" step could look like a second model switch, so the camera holds on the message box showing Opus.
  • Where they disagreed: Astra wanted Opus to ask "Could a lost reply cause a second charge?". Sol pointed out that hands Astra the answer. v2 uses Sol's open question, so the idea is clearly Astra's.
  • Both: the timing added up to 47 s, and scene 2 was too crowded. v2 has fewer lines and is about 45 s.

Your call: a third agent?

Both reviewers would keep GPT-6.1-Sol out of this video and give it its own short clip: Opus asks Sol to review the fix, and Sol catches something Opus then fixes. Adding Sol here makes the video about 8 s longer and repeats the picker.

Rules for the edit

  • Every line is plain English, about 12 words or fewer.
  • The key line holds 4 s and the others 3 s after they finish, with the camera still.
  • Captions say the point of the scene in plain words.
  • Labels only where the chat can't say it, and never over text being read.
  • Every step is something the app really does.
