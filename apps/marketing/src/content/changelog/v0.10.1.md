  - title: The Linux app starts on older systems again
    description: The 0.9.0 and 0.10.0 AppImages failed at startup on Ubuntu 24.04 and 22.04 and on Debian 12 and 13, with an error about a missing pty.node file. The terminal part had been built for a newer Linux than those systems run. It works on Ubuntu 22.04 and newer again, and each release now opens a terminal on Ubuntu 22.04 before it ships. If your app won't start, download 0.10.1 from threadlines.dev.
    evidence:
      - "50dd3921"
  - title: Sharing a theme keeps your email private
    description: A theme you share to the Showcase now carries your GitHub login and GitHub's no-reply address, not your email. The share window says the share is public right away, and under which account. While a share is in review, Take back from Showcase in the theme's menu removes it, and its tile then reads "Taken back from the Showcase".

      - "b02b8c46"
